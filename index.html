About

Hey I’m Tobi — Wabu Games

I’m a Black (Nigerian) British game developer with a deep passion for writing, storytelling, and games that stay with players long after the credits roll.

I graduated with a First Class Honours in BA Games Design & Art from the University of Southampton and am currently pursuing a Master’s Degree in Games Design at the University of Southern California.

I was recently awarded the Gerald A. Lawson Fund by PlayStation Career Pathways and am now part of that program too.

Through my work I aim to build games where mechanics and narrative reinforce each other — stories you don’t just watch, but experience through play.

  • Working on LOVANIA, another personal project of mine...
